# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **Porto Faifo Sans** adds SemiBold 600 and ExtraBold 800 in the static, Optical, and variable builds. Black 900 refines the lowercase `a`, the numeral `5`, and Vietnamese circumflexes. Figures are now proportional by default. Use `tnum` for equal-width figures in tables.
- **Porto Faifo Mono** aligns vertical metrics and underlines across Regular and Medium.
- **Both families** expand Latin coverage with `Ŋ`, `ŋ`, `ʼ`, and combining macron below. Font files now use the `PortoFaifoSans` and `PortoFaifoMono` family names. The specimen displays all seven Sans weights.
- **Porto Faifo Symbols** includes the 322 SVG symbols and sprites in the 1.1.0 package.

| --- | --- |
| `PFSans-1.1.0.zip` | OTF, TTF, WOFF2, Optical, and variable fonts |

## 1.0.0

The first release of Porto Faifo, 26 November 2026. Porto Faifo is the new name of KÉT Sans,
KÉT Sans Mono and Két Symbol; the letterforms are those of KÉT Sans 1.1 and KÉT Sans Mono 0.10.4.

- **Porto Faifo Sans**: Light, Regular, Medium, Bold, Black; optical-size and variable fonts.
- **Porto Faifo Mono**: Regular, Medium.
- **Porto Faifo Symbols**: 322 symbols at 14, 16, 20 and 24 px in four weights, as SVG files and sprites.
- File names start with `PF`; sprite ids start with `pf-`.
